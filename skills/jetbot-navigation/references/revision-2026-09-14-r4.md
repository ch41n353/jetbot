# Revision 4 — 2026-09-14, offline iteration

The user prohibited robot movement while away and requested simulation validation
and a morning report. This iteration used no live camera, IMU or motor service.
Report: `/home/jetbot/jetbot/reports/2026-09-14-morning.md`.

New reusable tools, all offline:

- `local_nav/simulate_route.py`: production route executor, real carpet tracker
  and planar estimator, rendered fisheye carpet, independent simulated motor
  dynamics/ground truth and expiring leases. Socket, camera, IMU attitude and
  clock are mocked. Parameters are hypotheses, not robot calibration.
- `local_nav/simulation_stress.py`: longer goals, delayed processing, low texture,
  exposure/read noise, stall, camera freeze, sensor failure, cancellation, IMU
  drift and incorrect camera-height scenarios, at 250 and 125 features.
- `local_nav/benchmark_tracker.py`: fixed recorded-pair timing and outputs.
- `local_nav/evaluate_feature_budget.py`: 250/125/80 corner-budget comparisons.
- `local_nav/evaluate_braking_candidate.py`: separate-seed comparison of an
  80 ms coasting hypothesis. This candidate is not deployed in the controller.

Read `/home/jetbot/jetbot/local_nav/SIMULATION.md` for commands and limitations.

Production changes: optional `route_executor.py --feature-budget 125` (80 also
supported experimentally), default remains 250. Float32 image-normalization
intermediates reduce buffer size; on the 41-pair comparison tracker outputs were
identical, but timing differences alone do not establish a reliable speedup.
The settling history now spans up to 400 ms so three frames at 170–180 ms spacing
can establish settling. Minimum stable duration and displacement thresholds are
unchanged. An exhausted post-stop window now has an explicit reason.

Results: on 60 paired 5 cm simulations, predictive braking reduced median true
absolute error from 2.09 to 0.69 cm; 45/60 ended within 1 cm, with 15 overshoots.
On 16 held-out cases, the offline 80 ms candidate ended within 1 cm in 14 cases
versus 9 for the existing predictor. These are simulated populations, not real
robot success rates. The paired campaign is frozen at source commit `53d12ee`.

Reduced-feature replay: 95 pairs, no rejection with either 125 or 80 features;
max translation difference from 250 features was 0.0147 cm / 0.0254 cm. The two
250-feature timing baselines differed, so report their range rather than an
unqualified speedup. No physical validation of reduced features was performed.

Known limitation: a 15% camera-height error can produce `goal_reached` with more
than 1 cm of true simulated error. Settling and residual quality cannot establish
absolute monocular metric scale. Keep this outcome framed as an estimated arrival;
do not reduce clearance or authorize autonomous operation based on these numbers.
