The object mission now attempts bounded local recovery for geometric floor-fit

existing estimator and adds uncertainty for the gap; no position reset occurs.

conservative stopping envelope (uncertain-pivot sweeps through±20degrees expanded

change or unmeasurable stopping movement still stops the mission. This new recovery
