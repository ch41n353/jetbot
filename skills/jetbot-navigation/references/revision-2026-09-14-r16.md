# Revision 16 — shallow approaches within local alignment scope

Date: 2026-09-14. Local changes only; no GitHub push.

For missions using local floor alignment, the goal is now a reachable point on
the standoff circle rather than necessarily the radial point facing the object.
The selected approach heading is limited to seven degrees, reserving three
degrees inside the existing ten-degree measured-yaw stop guard. An unreachable
standoff within that heading range is rejected.

Such missions require a clear direct corridor and checked starting turn envelope.
The controller follows the direct goal from the beginning instead of a coarse
waypoint sequence that may postpone lateral movement until too late. It clamps
the desired absolute heading to seven degrees and damps measured yaw rate. All
existing map, power, tilt, timing, target identity, world consistency, recovery,
and measured-yaw guards remain. The preview shows the entire direct approach
envelope; full-frame rectification remains disabled.

The simulator case with target [-18,100] cm, a four-degree synthetic camera bias,
matching local correction, and projective contact tracking reached a true range
of 20.75 cm in 8.30 simulated seconds. It used zero intermediate model calls and
recorded no clearance violations or watchdog stops; final motor output was zero.
See `reports/iteration-2026-09-14/shallow-approach-simulation.json`.
Three aligned-mission tests passed, including unreachable-goal rejection and
uncompensated-camera-bias rejection. This change has not yet been live validated.
