# Revision 15 — contact tracking, local floor alignment, and completion recovery

Date: 2026-09-14. Changes remain local; do not push without an explicit request.

## Failure diagnosis

Repeated approaches stopped when the tracked bottle's floor projection disagreed
with accumulated carpet odometry. High appearance confidence did not establish
an accurate contact point. The appearance box's bottom drifted approximately
three pixels above the inspected bottle contact in a recorded approach.

Independent floor-only rigidity fitting on 41 moving frame pairs favored a
minus-four-degree camera-frame pitch correction. Median residual objectives
improved on both training and held-out halves: mean per-pair residual decreased
from 0.0521 to 0.0288 cm on training and 0.0613 to 0.0349 cm on validation.
Homography decompositions also indicated a camera/floor orientation discrepancy.
These observations do not distinguish an IMU/camera mounting error from a local
floor slope and do not establish a new global calibration.

## Optional mission inputs

`projective_contact: true` enables a separate projective contact-point tracker.
The existing immutable appearance identity and bounding-box tracker remain in
place. Contact propagation requires distributed features, sufficient homography
inliers, a bounded positive local Jacobian, and a point near the verified target
boundary. Ambiguous identity, excessive motion, and invalid geometry still fail.

`floor_alignment_rotation` optionally supplies a proper 3x3 camera-frame rotation
of at most eight degrees. The mission uses it consistently for the frozen preview
attitude and the execution timeline's camera projection. The current local trial
used a Rodrigues vector `[-4, 0, 0]` degrees. This is a scene-specific experimental
input, not a default to reuse in another room, patch, or heading. Such missions
stop beyond ten degrees of measured mission yaw. Raw IMU reference gravity,
relative tilt checks, and calibration files on disk remain unchanged.

`floor_alignment_evidence` records the supporting report path in the plan.
The image and contact must still be selected from a fresh capture. The full map,
chassis clearance, power guard, timing limits, and world-consistency guard remain.

Together, the new contact tracker and the independently selected floor correction
passed the existing world-consistency guard on all 44 recorded approach frames;
the original implementation violated it on 34. The guard is range dependent
(`max(5 cm, 10% of local range)`); this far-target replay does not imply error was
always below five centimetres. Seven tracker tests passed. Production replay
matched the offline contact coordinates exactly, with median 22.04 ms and maximum
31.75 ms for tracking plus JPEG decode. Five capture/alignment tests passed.
Two additional end-to-end simulator tests verify that a four-degree camera
attitude bias stops an uncompensated mission before motion, while the matching
scene-scoped correction and projective contact tracker reach the simulated target
without intermediate model calls or clearance violations. This exercises the
combined options but does not independently validate the physical calibration.

## Straight steering and stopped recovery

The straight route executor now caps desired lateral-correction heading at
1.5 degrees and damps measured yaw rate using a 100 ms angular lookahead. Existing
heading gain, motor correction limits, and the five-degree heading guard remain.
Thirty-six focused route, steering, braking, map, and simulator tests passed.

Floor recovery first commands zero motors and collects stopped observations.
Before accepting the bridge, it checks a coast envelope from the complete measured
IMU yaw history, with two degrees of angular allowance, the existing unknown-pivot
sweep, and a full four-centimetre translation allowance. A fixed hypothetical
plus/minus-twenty-degree sweep previously rejected even a small observed coast.
Missing, nonfinite, unbracketed, or gapped IMU history rejects recovery. Existing
maximum twenty-degree excursion, four-centimetre bridge, quiet-frame, tilt,
cancellation, deadline, floor-fit, and uncertainty checks remain. Eleven focused
recovery tests plus one arrival-failure integration test passed (12 total). The
simulated braking failure recovered in 0.55 seconds, completed in 5.26 seconds,
and ended at a true simulated range of 18.48 cm. Recovery issued no powered motor
commands; the run recorded no clearance violations or watchdog stops. It used the
simulator's existing sampled plant yaw, rate, and derived acceleration history.
Do not claim powered validation of this latest fix yet.

## Reusable offline alignment fitting

`local_nav/fit_floor_alignment.py OBSERVATIONS OUTPUT_CANDIDATE` fits a bounded
camera-frame pitch correction from recorded carpet motion. It selects the
correction using the training portion's floor rigidity objective and reports
held-out metrics separately. It never uses the target distance as a fitting
objective, writes the global calibration, or operates hardware. A candidate is
scene-specific and must pass independent target/geometry checks before use.
Four focused tests passed; replaying the 46-frame recording selected minus four
degrees from 41 moving pairs. See `fitted-floor-alignment.json`.

## Full-frame fisheye rectification comparison

The production pipeline still tracks features in the raw image and undistorts
their coordinates for metric geometry. Full-frame rectification was evaluated
offline using cached remap tables and a proper pinhole ray model; setting zero
fisheye distortion coefficients would not implement that model.

Both floor pipelines passed all 132 frame pairs over three repeats. Rectification
added approximately 14.5 ms/frame; floor processing including remap increased
from 46.54 to 61.00 ms median. The final forward estimates differed by 0.12 cm.
Both target trackers also tracked all 132 frames. Raw target processing took
11.32 ms median versus 26.41 ms including rectification. The rectified contact
was farther from the manually inspected contact in all three checked images;
lower target/world residual therefore does not establish improved accuracy.

Full-frame rectification was not enabled. This replay covers a mostly central
target, not peripheral objects. `local_nav/evaluate_target_rectification.py`
preserves the target comparison; `rectification-target-ab.json` and
`rectification-floor-ab.json` contain evidence. The floor comparison script is
saved alongside those reports as `floor_rectification_ab.py`.
