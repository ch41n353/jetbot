## Service and motor path

`local_nav/service.py` runs camera (~30 Hz), IMU (~40–43 Hz), and an independent
motor worker. Private socket: `/tmp/jetbot-local-nav/control.sock`; lock prevents
duplicate instances. Service starts disarmed unless `--enable-motion` is supplied.

Each motor lease lasts 200 ms. Motor worker checks roughly every 10 ms and calls
the driver only when desired output changes. `core.allowed` requires finite
speeds ≤0.3 magnitude, issued age ≤250 ms, unexpired lease no longer than 250 ms,
camera age ≤500 ms, and IMU age ≤200 ms. Camera/IMU unhealthy state rejects new
commands; outstanding commands expire. Worker cleanup stops motors, but a wedged
driver, system freeze, or SIGKILL is not a hardware-guaranteed cutoff.

Actions:

- `status`: current sensor health, timestamps, readings, motion-enabled flag,
  and latest motor-worker report.
- `snapshot`: returns a freshly written JPEG path only if the camera is current.
- `observation`: selected frame bytes (`jpeg_base64`), frame timestamp and recent
  IMU history. Avoids racing over a replaced JPEG file. Host acquisition timestamps
  are not hardware synchronization; exposure/IMU time offset is uncalibrated.
- `motors`: legacy behavior sends an invalid command to revoke the old lease,
  then a validated new one. Appropriate to the existing pulse tool; repeated
  renewal can interrupt output.
- `motors_hold`: validates and replaces the lease **without an intervening stop**.
  Used by current smooth probes. Invalid requests still revoke through the error
  handler. Repeating an unchanged desired output does not repeatedly reprogram
  the motor driver. The lease/watchdog remains active.
- `stop`: revoke motor lease, but does not cancel a client controller.
- `shutdown`: revoke, stop service and workers; prevents further client renewal.

## Attitude and floor motion

`state_estimator.py` subtracts calibrated gyro bias, integrates gravity direction
with Rodrigues rotations and gyro yaw about gravity. Initial gravity must be
within 4° of the calibrated starting pose. Rotation magnitude >90°/s or IMU gaps
>80 ms stop estimation. Acceleration outside 3–20 m/s² stops immediately; values
outside 7–13 m/s² are rejected as gravity, and sustained excursions ≥80 ms stop.
Slow gravity correction uses near-1g, low-gyro samples and small disagreement.

Frame attitude is interpolated; at most 35 ms gyro extrapolation is allowed.
`settled_at` can replace frame gravity using a preceding 180 ms window with ≥6
samples spanning ≥120 ms, gyro ≤1.5°/s, acceleration per-axis std ≤0.12 m/s² and
norm within 0.35 m/s² of 9.80665. Gravity disagreement >2° stops. It leaves yaw
unchanged. Stop-and-observe requires quiet windows on both sides of a motion pair,
waiting up to 180 ms extra; it does not mix corrected and drifting floor planes.

`FloorTracker`:

1. Grayscale, subtract mean brightness of floor ROI and add a common offset to
   reduce global exposure-change failures.
2. Up to 250 corners, ROI x160–480/y280–460. Pyramidal LK (21×21, max level 3),
   optionally initialized by gravity-derived camera tilt. Forward/back error <0.8 px.
3. Undistort fisheye rays and intersect the gravity-defined floor at camera height.
4. RANSAC partial affine fit, 0.35 cm threshold, ≥20 inliers and ≥65% inlier ratio.
   Apparent scale must stay strictly within **0.97–1.03**.
5. Normalize fitted rotation, fuse visual yaw with gyro yaw; disagreement >3°
   stops. Median translation is refitted with fused rotation. Residual ≤0.3 cm,
   translation magnitude ≤5 cm per accepted frame pair.

Planar state integrates visual translation and fused heading, estimates velocity,
and accumulates heuristic variance. It does not double-integrate acceleration
into position, perform SLAM, or provide calibrated statistical error bounds.

## Current motion algorithms

### Smooth straight travel (`smooth_drive_probe.py`)

Acquire a valid pair before driving. At each new frame: use raw propagated frame
attitude, fit floor motion, accumulate path length and yaw, check limits, then
renew `motors_hold` at `left=.16+steer`, `right=.16-steer`, with
`steer=clip(-.3*yaw_radians, -.025, .025)`. No programmed settling stop inside the
segment. Mean measured loop rate in an earlier probe was ~10.7 Hz; not guaranteed.

Stop when requested travel threshold (1–15 cm) is crossed **or** powered time
reaches two seconds, with three seconds total loop budget. Stop on >180 ms frame
or renewal delay, >5° heading deviation, or estimator/tracker failure. Finally
issue stop. There is no autonomous obstacle check or separate progress/stall
detector in this probe. `bounded_probe_completed` does not distinguish distance
from time completion. Travel is path length, not necessarily forward displacement.

Implementation caveat: `frame()` still calls `settled_at()` before the smooth
probe replaces its returned attitude with `timeline.at()`. Thus the helper can
still raise a settled-gravity disagreement even though smooth fitting uses raw
attitude. This is an unresolved coupling, not a claim of fully independent VIO.

Threshold overshoot of roughly 0.5–1.2 cm has been observed, plus unmeasured
braking motion. Keep that outside the required obstacle margin. `drive_elapsed`
includes some stop-call overhead and is not an electrical motor-on measurement.

### General turn (`turn_controller.py`)

Target ±30° maximum, right positive. Power 0.12–0.16 (default .12). Issue 60 ms
differential pulse, explicit stop, wait 180 ms; observe camera/IMU between pulses.
Stop within 1.5° in the commanded direction; wrong-direction motion beyond 3°,
angle beyond requested magnitude +5°, tilt >5°, frame interval >650 ms, no motion
≥1° after three seconds, or 12-second timeout stops the trial. Existing estimator
90°/s guard also applies. No final independent angle measurement is performed.

### Smooth-turn experiment (`smooth_turn_probe.py`)

Fixed **15° right**, power .16, uses fresh status IMU samples (~40 Hz) and
`motors_hold`. Stops when `15-angle <= max(1.5°, positive_rate * .05 seconds)`.
Limits: two seconds, renewal gap 150 ms, initial progress ≥1° after 400 ms,
rolling ≥0.5° progress over 300 ms after 500 ms, angle -2°..20°, tilt 5°,
and estimator guards. Stop then collect 250 ms of settling IMU data to report
final angle. Successful trial: **16.28°**, powered interval **0.273 s**.
This is one bounded trial, not a calibrated general-angle braking model.

### Slow floor-point controller (`point_controller.py`)

Initial pixel projects 30–40 cm forward, lateral magnitude <5 cm, checked against
both static geometry and quiet measured attitude. Uses 100 ms forward pulses,
base .20, bearing steering gain .30 capped ±.05, settling .25–.5 s plus loop work.
Stop within configurable 10–20 cm of waypoint. Limits include 20 s, 40 cm travel,
20° total heading, 7° incremental yaw, 0.3-radian bearing corridor, 900 ms frame
interval, uncertainty checks, sensor and tracking checks. It is not the default
fast object approach, and a waypoint standoff is not an object clearance.

## Results and failures that must survive iteration

- Earlier low-power continuous turn attempts stalled or timed out. Do not repeat
  12-second low-progress trials or assert that lack of motion is software-only.
  The user expressed concern about motor damage; none was independently assessed.
- 0.16 pulses restored movement when 0.12 failed; a 10° request measured 8.71°
  in 1.22 s. Three 30° right pulse turns totaled 91.39° by IMU. Higher power has
  also triggered the 90°/s rate guard; 0.14 was used on subsequent approaches.
- Continuous forward steps of ~15–16 cm took ~1.2–1.6 s in several clear-carpet
  trials. Astra/image inspection between steps still adds substantial wall time.
- The Advil approach reached an estimated ~13 cm camera clearance in an earlier
  batch. Those estimates were not externally measured.
- A prior route incorrectly treated camera clearance as chassis clearance and
  called the offscreen Advil "behind" when the user reported it directly to the
  camera's right. This prompted the confirmed footprint and 5 cm margin.
- Full-width overlays and offscreen obstacle retention are high-level practices;
  they are **not enforced by the controllers**. Some recorded overlays project
  only straight translation and omit the near-camera/rear portion. Turn sweep,
  pose uncertainty, and obstacle-map persistence remain unfinished engineering.
- User repositioning invalidates old route coordinates. Fresh views have shown
  large resets between tasks. Do not carry a last-known pose blindly across one.
- Service sockets have disappeared between calls; reason not established. Check
  for session exit versus hardware failure rather than inventing a diagnosis.
- At skill capture, the latest Coke approach had completed a wide detour and a
  final 15.62 cm straight segment. The Coke can was visible ahead. A proposed last
  five-cm step was **not executed** because the service was unavailable. Do not
  report that final arrival or resume it automatically on loading this skill.

and swept-footprint collision checks shared by previews and execution. Keep side
and rear obstacles when they leave view. Improve continuous-drive progress and
braking checks, unify helper attitude behavior, and validate measured distances
and turns independently. Record improvements as a new dated revision rather than
