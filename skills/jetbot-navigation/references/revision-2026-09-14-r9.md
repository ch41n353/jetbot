# Revision 9 — battery-pack monitor and offline map validation

Date:2026-09-14, America/Los_Angeles. Revision8 remains as the earlier checkpoint.

## Battery monitoring now includes the pack

After the input-only implementation, a read-only probe found a second device at
bus1/address0x41 with INA219-compatible configuration0x399f. Its bus-voltage
register read12.276V, distinct from the Jetson's roughly5V input rail. This
matches the battery monitor used by the Waveshare three-cell JetBot board.
The board model has not been explicitly confirmed by the user; the3S profile
is an inference from the device, voltage and matching manufacturer hardware.

`battery.py` now requires BOTH the INA3221 input monitor and this pack monitor
in the production service. Missing pack telemetry cannot silently fall back
to the regulated5V rail. It checks continuous conversion, completed conversion
and unchanged configuration and performs no configuration/calibration writes.
Pack voltage uses the documented4mV bus-register LSB; INA3221 input voltage
uses8mV. Separate acquisition times are retained, and freshness uses the older
of the two readings.

Current experiment policy:

| Reading | Warning | Latched stop |
|---|---|---|
| Battery pack, inferred3S profile | below11.4V | below10.8V or above12.9V |
| Regulated Jetson input | below4.9V | below4.8V or above5.25V |
| Required telemetry | — | invalid, missing, or older than0.6seconds |

These are conservative experiment thresholds, not a state-of-charge curve or
a replacement for the battery protection circuit. Waveshare recommends
recharging its JetBot below roughly10V; the experimental stop is deliberately
earlier. Confirm the pack profile if the user identifies different hardware.

`battery_percent` remains null. No trustworthy percentage, remaining runtime,
discharge current or charging-state estimator is implemented. The INA219's
current/power calibration register was zero at inspection; do not treat zero
current as evidence that the battery is not discharging. Charger connection
is also not established by a healthy pack-voltage reading.

The existing approximately5Hz worker, independent motor interlock, stale-data
deadline and stop latch remain. `status.power` includes `pack_voltage_v`,
minimum pack voltage, timestamps, thresholds and policy revision. Both drive
and turn logs record start/end power status. The supervisor emits power state
changes and blocks further route launches after a power fault.

Hardware checks were stationary/disarmed. Combined monitor readings were about
12.24–12.29V at the pack and4.984–5.08V at the regulated input. The integrated
service returned motor output[0,0], motion_enabled=false, and shut down cleanly.
The test suite passed106 tests, including a low-pack stop while the regulated
rail remained healthy and a non-restarting independent motor watchdog.

Sources: [Waveshare JetBot battery guidance](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/JetBot_AI_Kit),
[manufacturer schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/1/16/JetBot.pdf),
[TI INA219 register units and conversion status](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina219.pdf).

The earlier battery report coincided with a reboot. Old live route anchors and
maps must not be resumed as though the robot retained its old service or pose.
Live navigation remains paused; a pack-voltage reading does not show that the
charger cable is disconnected or that the robot was not repositioned.

## Measured-map drive evaluation

`evaluate_spatial.py` runs paired production-controller simulations. In
`reports/iteration-2026-09-14/spatial-matrix.json`,72 runs cover36 paired cases:
offset return, narrow out-and-back, and combined turning/driving. Speed, coast,
camera rate, latency, pivot, drive-yaw bias and camera-height scale vary.

The fixed-rectangle method completed20/36; the opt-in measured-map adapter
completed36/36. Maximum ground-truth error among successful runs was3.061cm.
No full-chassis-plus5cm boundary violation was observed by the independent
2ms plant audit. Median total local search time was about8.3ms for the new
adapter. There were no intermediate model calls.

This comparison used open floor and corridor boundaries, not rendered moving
objects. Obstacle-layout and sensor-fault checks are still separate work. The
simulator directly supplies IMU attitudes and does not model all carpet/motor
effects. Keep `measured_map_drive` opt-in until further validation; this evidence
does not establish general autonomous obstacle avoidance or prove live behavior.
