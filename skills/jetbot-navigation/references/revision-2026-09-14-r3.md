# Revision 3 — 2026-09-14

`route_executor.py --predictive-braking` adds an experimental early stop based on
forward velocity, camera age and frame interval. The lead is capped at 2 cm, with
an initial 40 ms coasting estimate. Existing duty, distance, corridor, lease and
stall limits remain unchanged. Omitting the flag preserves revision-2 behavior.

After braking, the controller observes for at most one second without renewing
motors. At least three distinct post-stop frames spanning 180 ms with no more
than 0.10 cm coordinate variation are required to report settling. Arrival is
reported only when the final estimated forward position is within 1 cm of the
requested target and motor output is zero. It distinguishes stopping short,
overshoot and unverified final position. Estimator uncertainty and braking remain
experimental; this is not independent ground truth or obstacle detection.

Raw camera/IMU observations are saved in `<log>.observations` for replay. Powered
interval excludes the post-stop observation window. Failed post-stop tracking
must not be reported as arrival; no automatic corrective motor pulse is issued.

Evidence: 55 offline tests passed, including early braking, continued coasting,
stopping short/overshooting, duplicate-frame rejection, and no motor renewal after
braking. The revision-2 live 5 cm trial crossed at 5.776 cm in 0.659 seconds; it did
not measure final coasting. Live predictive-braking trial: 5 cm requested, brake command at 4.218 cm,
settled position 5.340 cm (error +0.340 cm), powered interval 0.935 seconds.
The 0.264-second settling window passed and motor output was verified zero.
This is one trial, not a statistical accuracy claim or measured speedup.
Evidence: `local_nav/goals/braking-live-2026-09-14-01.result.json`.

An explicitly user-authorized turnaround completed six bounded 30-degree steps
at duty 0.14, totaling 177.34 degrees by summed IMU estimates. Raw observation
replay estimated lens displacement [2.45, -12.44] cm and yaw 175.68 degrees;
this was used to retain the offscreen Coke position for the next route. A rough
axle-offset fit gave median 6.71 cm from 74 moving frame pairs, with substantial
scatter. It is not installed as a measured calibration.
